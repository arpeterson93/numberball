League parity

Each line is one season, by games played. Hover a line to isolate it, and the win-total chart at the bottom shows that season at that point. All of these follow the seasons filter above. One extreme team, like a 0-9 team, can swing the first chart a lot, which is why the third and fourth exist.

Spread of win percentage versus luck

How spread out the teams' win percentages are, divided by how spread out they would be if every game were a coin flip. 1.0 means the league looks like chance, and higher means real separation between teams.

Spread of run differential per game

How spread out the teams' average run margins are, in runs per game. Lower means a flatter league. Scores carry more signal than wins and losses, so this is the steadier read in a short season.

Spread without the best and worst team

The first chart again, but ignoring the single best and single worst team so one extreme record cannot swing it. 1.0 is chance, and the chance level is adjusted for the trim.

Share of teams near .500

The share of teams within a win and a half of .500, such as 3 to 6 wins after 9 games. The dashed line is what chance gives. Higher means a more bunched-up league.

Teams by win total

Hover any line above to see how many teams had each win total for that season at that point. It starts on Season 13's latest point.
