Plays data audit

Sweeps every play across every season in docs/data/{s01..sNN,current}/plays_*.json - the same files the Gameday viewer itself loads, built from the MLN Google Sheets - and checks that baserunners, outs, and score flow correctly from one play to the next within each game, that every game's plays are numbered without gaps, and that no whole sessions are missing. Entirely client-side: no database connection, just the site's own already-published data. Click a row to see the surrounding plays in that game.
