# Timer proposal replay: "timer + dead window"

Question (Nov 2026): what if each hitter/pitcher picks a daily 4-hour dead window that doesn't count against their timer,
and the timer drops to 8 hours (8+4, max wait 12h = today's cap)? Built from every timed wait in the stadium thread dumps
(S11-S13, `tools/pace_league.py` -> `pa_detail.csv`). Regenerate with `python -I tools/pace_timer_proposal.py`.

Today's timer is 12h (the 18 autos in the data land at ~12h). Savings below are against that, with waits capped at 12h
(194 waits ran past 12h - ump delays or pauses, not looked into). 16,320 waits (8,161 batter, 8,159 pitcher), 456 players,
151 games, ~30,000h of waiting (the script's 30,009h uses whole-minute capping; early write-ups said 30,125h).

## Findings

- Waits are mostly short: median 0.56h (batters 1.18h, pitchers 0.19h); 59% finish inside an hour but those are only 8% of
  the waiting hours. Waits of 8h+ are 6% of waits and ~32% of hours, but that is not the saving - a capped wait still takes 8h.
- 8+4 saves ~440h (1.5% of waiting, ~3h per game), cuts 214-350 waits (1.3-2.1%). A plain 8h timer saves 2,020h (6.7%) and cuts
  945 waits; 6+0 saves 4,554h (15.1%), 6+6 saves ~1,260h (4.2%, 577 waits).
- Why 8+4 saves so little: 70% of the 945 waits over 8h contain the player's whole dead window (they are overnight waits), and a
  wait spanning the window gets 8+4 = 12h, same as today. Only waits that miss the window (mostly started 8am-8pm) are cut; those
  281 daytime-start waits are 375 of the 441 hours saved.
- At a fixed max wait, more dead time and a shorter counted timer saves more (8h total: 8+0 2,020h, 7+1 2,379h, 6+2 2,968h;
  12h total: 8+4 441h, 7+5 716h, 6+6 1,260h) at the cost of more cut waits. See `grid_*.csv` / `grid_both.png`.
- It does not target the games that drag: correlation of game length and trim is 0.06; the longest 15 games (~11.7 days of
  waiting) are trimmed 1.8% vs 1.4% for the rest. Long games have more PAs (~68 vs ~53), not a different wait mix.
  Teams range from 0.1% (KC) to 2.9% (BBEG) trimmed, small samples (`per_*_8+4.csv`).
- Behaviour matters more than last-minute answers (`behaviour_scenarios.csv`): if counted time past 4h shrinks 10% (k=0.9) 8+4
  saves ~870h (2.9%); at 25% (k=0.75) ~1,470h (4.9%) with autos halved (214 -> 106). These k/p values are assumptions, not
  measured; calibrating them needs a past timer change.

## Method notes

- Wait = batter's ping -> swing, or the pitcher's gap before the next ping minus the estimated ump relay (a no-ping result ends at
  the result). Batter waits with no swing post are estimates.
- A player's dead window = their own quietest D-hour block (fewest wait ends), league window if under 12 waits. Real players may
  choose better or worse. If everyone used one fixed window, 8+4 would save 394-1,783h depending on the hour.
- Batter and pitcher names are separate entities (a person playing both gets two windows). Nobody is assumed to respond faster
  in the static replay, so those numbers are a floor.
- Not generated by the script (one-off analyses): the hour-of-day split (35% of the excess hours over 8h fall in 8am-8pm CT;
  waits starting 8pm-8am hold 64% of the hours beyond 8h) and the behavior-adjusted histogram chart.
